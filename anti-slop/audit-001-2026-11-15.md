## Overall read

The site is far from typical slop. One accent, a real wordmark, real social links, working keyboard and reduced-motion support, empty/loading/error states all present, no fake stats or testimonials, no em dashes in source. The risk is the opposite failure (Part 3): a competent but _sterile_ dark UI. Findings below lean that way.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-39: no `cursor: pointer` in the base CSS.** `globals.css` never sets it. Tailwind 4 preflight resets buttons to `cursor: default`, so every `<button>` (menu toggle, locale toggle, pause, retry, delete, admin forms) and `summary` ("Show older shows") feels dead on desktop. No `cursor: not-allowed` for disabled controls either. Fix: one base-layer rule covering `a[href], button:not(:disabled), summary, select, label[for], [role=button|tab|switch]` plus the disabled rule.
2. **R-38 / R-23: placeholder-looking contact email shipped as a real default.** `src/config/site.ts` has `contactEmail: "info@bandland.com"` (and the comment says "Add your booking/press contact email here"). It is the `mailto:` for "Booking & press" whenever settings fail to load. Needs confirmation that it is real, otherwise swap for the band's actual address.
3. **R-39: a single link wrapped in a `<nav>` landmark.** `HomeFeatures` merch widget renders `<nav aria-label="Explore"><a>Merch</a></nav>`. A one-link nav landmark is noise for screen-reader landmark lists and the header already has the real nav. Fix: plain link or section, no landmark.
4. **R-39: ARIA/semantics check on the mobile menu.** Closed menu items use `tabIndex={-1}` instead of being removed from the tab order and accessibility tree (`hidden`/`inert`). Worth confirming with a screen reader that closed content is not announced. Verify, then fix if confirmed.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate / liveliness)

5. **R-37 / Part 3: no Design Read, no dials.** There is no `DESIGN.md`, and nothing declares ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION. Proposed: _Reading this as: band landing for fans and bookers, dark monochrome with one red accent, ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1._ Fix: add a short `DESIGN.md` with that line and the identity motif.
6. **R-05 / R-14 / Uniform Section Rhythm: home page below the hero is "two identical panels".** Next show and Release are the same `panel p-6 sm:p-8` with kicker + h2 + muted line, in a 2-col grid. Merch is a bare link tile of a different weight. Shows and Merch pages are intro + list. Fix: give the next show a different composition from the release (e.g. big tabular date as the focal point, release embed full-bleed), written reason per section.
7. **R-20 / swap test: identity lives only in the hero photos and the wordmark.** Below the fold, panels, rounded-lg buttons, red primary are generic dark UI. Swap the wordmark and it could be any band. Candidate motif from the content itself: the blackletter + the Afrikaans voice ("Skollie-gees"), e.g. the date block or section titles treated as a consistent gesture. Needs your input (see questions).
8. **R-06 / eyebrow labels: `section-kicker` repeats the title.** "Live" above "Shows", "Store" above "Merch", "Gallery" above "Instagram", "Release" above a release title. Red text eyebrow that the heading already says. Fix: remove the kicker where it duplicates, keep it only when it adds info (e.g. "Next show" is useful, "Live / Shows" is not).
9. **R-08: decorative arrows.** `↗` on hero "Listen", release CTA and every merch "View"; `→` on the merch tile. `↗` as external-link signal is defensible but is repeated on every primary button; `→` on the merch tile is pure decoration. Fix: keep one convention (external-link marker on external links only) and drop the `→`.
10. **R-16 / copy: weak or leaky strings.**
    - Shows price guide fallback: "Shows may list one or both prices. If only a general price is available, it is shown without icons." This explains the UI's internals to fans. Rewrite or drop.
    - "Listen to the newest drop." and "Featured items and store links." are generic filler; "Newest release" repeats the section label.
    - Merch CTA "View" (R-15) does not say what happens. "Buy" or "Order" is what the link actually does, if that is true of the store links.
    - Afrikaans copy should be reviewed by a native speaker for tone parity with English.
11. **R-25 / R-35: unverified contrast and runtime.** Hero tagline and buttons sit on photos under a `bg-bg/35` plus bottom gradient. Token pairs (text, text-dim, highlight on bg) pass AA by calculation, but text over the actual photos was not sampled. Also not run: build, console errors, 320px layout, click-through. I can do all of this as a follow-up using the contrast checker and the dev server.
12. **R-10: backdrop blur on three elements.** Header (`backdrop-blur-md`), mobile menu overlay (`backdrop-blur-sm`), hero pause button (`backdrop-blur`). Cap is 1-2. Fix: drop the blur on the pause button (solid `bg-bg/80` is enough) and the overlay.
13. **R-01 / "Excessive Accent Color": red is used for kickers, prices, primary buttons, arrows, badge, header dot, selection, focus, hover and footer underline.** Single accent is the right call, but it is on ~10 roles, so it stops signalling anything. Fix: reserve it for the primary action, price and active state; kickers (see #8) lose it.

## LOW (Quality Locks / polish)

15. **R-11: radius scale spread.** `md`, `lg`, `xl`, `2xl`, `full` are all in use. Mostly coherent, but define the scale (control / card / pill-for-status-only) once in `globals.css`.
16. **R-06: Geist chosen without a written reason.** Geist is one of the listed AI-default sans picks. It may stay; the reason (neutral foil to the blackletter wordmark) just needs writing down in `DESIGN.md`.
